Questions

Do guests need an app?

No. Any iPhone from XS (2018) onwards and almost every Android phone reads NFC natively — they just hold the phone near the plaque. Older phones can scan the QR code printed on it.

Will an iPhone join the Wi-Fi automatically?

iPhones open the guide page and the password is copied with one tap. Android and iPhone users can also scan a Wi-Fi QR. We keep the plaque pointing to your guide so it never breaks when you change the password.

What happens if I change my Wi-Fi password?

Log in, change it, save. Every tag shows the new password immediately — nothing to reprint or reprogram.

What if I stop paying after 12 months?

Your plaque keeps working and shows a basic page with your Wi-Fi details. The full multi-section guide, stickers and statistics need the €29/year renewal.

Can I use it in several properties?

Yes — each kit is one property. The Host 5-Pack is designed for co-hosts and managers.

How do I pay?

PayPal (you can pay with a debit or credit card without a PayPal account) or SEPA bank transfer. Spanish customers can also use Bizum. Kits are made to order and ship within 3–5 working days after payment.

Is guest data collected?

No. We count taps anonymously per tag (no IP addresses, no cookies). See our privacy policy.
